overflow 

overflow: visible;   the default — it spills out, and stays readable
overflow: hidden;    clipped; no scrollbar, but a script can still scroll it
overflow: clip;      clipped; nothing can scroll it, not even a script
overflow: scroll;    scrollbars always, even with nothing to scroll
overflow: auto;      scrollbars only when they are needed
overflow-x 

overflow-x: hidden;
overflow-y: visible;    → quietly becomes auto

to clip one axis and leave the other alone, use clip
overflow-x: clip;
overflow-y: visible;    → stays visible
